# Disclaimer

**This is not a diagnostic tool.** It is a hobby data logger that records values the car's engine computer already reports. It has been verified on a single vehicle only.

- **Do not rely on it for safety.** It cannot tell you that a diesel particulate filter is blocked, failing, or safe to keep driving. The soot figure is the engine computer's own estimate, not a measurement of the filter. Some values may be missing, wrong for your car, or provisional (see [pids.md](pids.md) for what is verified and what is not).
- **Thresholds, colours and health alerts are the author's guesses.** The 20 hPa idle-pressure flag, the panel's green/amber/red soot bands and the regeneration flags have not been validated over a long history and are not manufacturer limits. The same is true of the DPF health tracking (oil-change reminder, "fast regens" alert, slow-warm-up alert): the regen-interval, oil-change-interval and warm-up thresholds behind them are the owner's own estimates, not manufacturer service intervals, and are not a substitute for your car's real maintenance schedule.
- **The next-regen mileage estimate is an estimate.** The figure on the screen and web page is extrapolated from this one car's soot-accumulation rate and the soot level its regens have started at. It is not an ECU value, it is hidden ("not soon") below 12 g of soot because it was too far out there, and it can still be well out above that.
- **Regeneration guidance is informational.** Messages such as "DO NOT SWITCH OFF UNTIL IT FINISHES" reflect general advice that interrupting a regeneration is undesirable. The flag behind them may be wrong or late. Follow your vehicle handbook, warning lights and a qualified mechanic, not this software. Never let this device distract you while driving.
- **The Telegram `/status` command gives an estimate, not a prediction you can rely on.** Sending `/status` to the bot (when Telegram is enabled) returns live figures plus a mileage-until-next-regen range inferred from this one car's own measured regen triggers and soot-accumulation rates (three observed regens at the time this was written). It is explicitly labelled `[INFERRED]` in the message, but it is still a rough extrapolation from very little data, for one vehicle, not a manufacturer figure or a guarantee of when (or whether) a regen will actually start.
- **Read-only, but at your own risk.** The code only requests readings and does not send clear-code, write or programming commands. Connecting any device to the OBD-II port, and any modification of firmware in a vehicle, is done at your own risk.
- **Trust your workshop's scanner** and judgement over this logger.
- **Security.** The web page on both builds has no authentication: anyone who can reach it on the network — your home WiFi, or the device's own `DPF-Sentinel` hotspot — can view live data, download logs, set the dash-mileage offset, and (where Telegram is configured) trigger a send. Change the hotspot password from the default (`WIFI_AP_PASS` in `include/secrets.h`) and do not forward its port to the internet. Keep Wi-Fi passwords, Telegram tokens and adapter addresses out of any published copy.
- **Compatibility.** Only the 2014 Hyundai ix35 1.7 CRDi has been tested. Other cars need their own discovery and verification.
